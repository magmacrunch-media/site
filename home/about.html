about

magmacrunch media, since 2023

"sometimes it's quite melodious; other times it's noise"

magmacrunch media is a record label and creative collective based in the United States (and beyond) that produces music, film, video games, web tools, writing, and more. Starting as YouTube channel in early 2023, the label began distributing music, new and old, to streaming platforms and online stores in mid 2024. The publishing arm magmacrunch music followed in 2025, and by 2026, the creation of this website led to the collective developing video games for the magmacrunch arcade. This also led to construction of the historical archive, the development of other ware, and the start of a DIY publishing press. In August 2026, magmacrunch media became a Pennsylvania LLC based in State College. Mostly recently, the collective started publishing iOS apps in November 2026.

As an organization, magmacrunch media is a label run by its own artists. There are no contracts and no advances to pay back, and everyone keeps ownership of their recordings, songwriting, and other creative works. Recording and production typically happen in home settings with a rotating cast of producers and musicians, all of them friends or family. A broader goal is to help people put out records they could never afford to make in a traditional studio.

The story of magmacrunch is perhaps best told in a non-linear fashion, by perusing the archive. Let's first take a step back to late 2015, in Iowa City, Iowa. The post space rock band Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome (THLD) was formed by a Venn diagram of brothers and physics grad school classmates. Members Jake, Jon, Stephen, and Judah would record music in the The Tuna Can. House inhabitent Jessica would attend their shows, frequently shouting "TEXASTOAST MAGMACRUNCH!", as if to grant the band an alter-ego. Amongst discussions of atomic physics, Stephen and Jake would often talk about the idea of a creative collective at the bar of the beloved Trumpet Blossom Cafe in Iowa City.

McCoy brothers Jake, James, and Jon grew up playing music at Frogwood Manor in a forested neighborhood of New Hampshire. Across the street was a young C.P. Rutledge, who grew up playing music at Spirit Castle. With D-Rob and other musicians living on the same block, this micro-scene was home to a host of bands and projects over the years, including Millstone, Vinny Bobarino, Juanito Thompson, The Four B's, Alien Hunting, and Bottle Boys, along with solo material by Brother Dynamo and Jon/Woah. School and work later took Jake and Jon to Iowa City, where they played in THLD and other associated projects. Meanwhile, James played in the Philadelphia-based band The Evergreen Drive with college friends at Temple.

Newer acts have since joined the long-time members, among them Dag Henderson and, most recently, Pennsylvania's Sex Van Floor Plan. Stephen, who played in Audio Sound Paper with Jake while THLD was active, went on to form Murder Finger with Jon, and Puma Man in the late 2010s, and most recently played in dogging in parked cars, a two-piece post-rock group in Spain. In addition to music and field recordings, he joined the magmacrunch development team along with Jake and James in 2026.

A soft spot for old technology runs through everything the collective makes, from releases that come out on obscure formats to video games that look like they belong to an earlier decade. The collective is currently branching out further into art and technology, with plans to build hardware, make documentaries, and publish various type of writing. As always, some of it will be melodious; some of it will be noise.
