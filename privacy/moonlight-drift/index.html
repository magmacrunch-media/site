// privacy //

moonlight drift for iphone and ipad

Moonlight Drift collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to magmacrunch media or to anyone else, except your Game Center scores and achievements, if you choose to sign in to Game Center.

This policy covers Moonlight Drift, the magmacrunch media game on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. It does not cover the magmacrunch.com website, where the browser version of the game has its own online scoreboard.

no network connections

The app makes no network connections of its own. Its graphics, fonts, sound effects and the song are all inside the app, and it plays the same with no internet connection at all. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no account to create. Unlike the browser version, the app has no online scoreboard: your best runs stay on your device.

what stays on your device

To work properly, the app remembers a few things on your device:

  • your best drifts and best sets, and the initials you entered for them
  • the pilot and the mode you last chose
  • how many pillars you have cleared in all, which decides which pilots are open
  • which Game Center achievements it has already reported, and which it still owes, so it does not announce one twice or lose one

The app also reads the date and time on your device, to draw the moon in tonight's phase. That stays on your device too.

None of this leaves your device through the app, and none of it identifies you. Like any app's data, it can be included in your device's own backups, which you control in your iPhone or iPad settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

game center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends the score of each finished run and your achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on the two leaderboards and on your Game Center profile. Apple handles that under Apple's privacy policy, and magmacrunch media does not receive it. Game Center is optional: the whole game works the same without signing in.

links

The app's credits link to pages about the software it is built on, on magmacrunch.com and capacitorjs.com. Following one opens your browser, outside the app.

children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, and the date below will change with it.
