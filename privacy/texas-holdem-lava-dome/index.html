// privacy //

texas hold'em lava dome for iphone and ipad

Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to magmacrunch media or to anyone else.

This policy covers Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome, the magmacrunch media game on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. It does not cover the magmacrunch.com website.

no network connections

The app makes no network connections of its own. Its cards, fonts and music are all inside the app, and it plays the same with no internet connection at all. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no account to create.

no real money

The chips are the game's own. They cannot be bought, sold, won or exchanged for anything, and the app has no purchases of any kind.

what stays on your device

To work properly, the app remembers a few things on your device:

  • your high scores, and the initials you entered for them
  • whether the music is on

None of it leaves your device through the app, and none of it identifies you. Like any app's data, it can be included in your device's own backups, which you control in your iPhone or iPad settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

links

The app's insert links to pages on magmacrunch.com. Following one opens your browser, outside the app.

children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, and the date below will change with it.
