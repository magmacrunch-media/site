// privacy //

gratinglab for iphone and ipad

gratinglab collects no data. Nothing you enter or compute is sent to magmacrunch media or to anyone else.

This policy covers gratinglab, the magmacrunch media diffraction calculator for iPhone and iPad. It does not cover the magmacrunch.com website, or the gratinglab Python package the app's solver is ported from.

no network connections

The app makes no network connections of its own. Everything it draws with, including its fonts, is inside the app, and it works the same with no internet connection at all. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no account to create.

The credits hold links to the magmacrunch media website and to the package's source. Tapping one opens it in Safari, outside the app, and that site's own policy then applies.

what stays on your device

The app remembers your setup between sessions:

  • the grating: its period, blaze angle and whether it has a gold coating
  • the light: its wavelength and the width of the band
  • the mount: its angles and roll, and which pair of angles you work in
  • what you were looking at: the view, which orders are shown, the reflectivity model, the Zoom setting, and whether the full-screen view was on

That is all. The camera position is deliberately not kept, and colour mode's light is not saved. The app keeps two copies of this information on your device, the second in its settings storage, so that iOS clearing space cannot reset it. Nothing here identifies you, neither copy leaves your device through the app, and deleting the app deletes both.

the clipboard

COPY puts the geometry and the table of orders on your clipboard as plain text, so you can paste it somewhere else. The app writes to your clipboard only when you press COPY, and it never reads your clipboard.

children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, and the date below will change with it.
