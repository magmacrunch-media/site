// privacy //

the magmacrunch.com website

This site has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and it does not sell anything about you. It only keeps what you choose to type into it, plus the ordinary logs a web server writes.

This policy covers magmacrunch.com and its browser games, run by magmacrunch media, LLC. Our iPhone and iPad apps have their own policies.

what you give us

  • Email list. If you sign up, we keep your address, when you signed up and which page you signed up from, in a private Google Sheet. We use it to send you magmacrunch news and for nothing else. Nobody has been sent anything yet. If you ask to leave, your address stays on the list marked as unsubscribed, so that we know not to write to you. Ask us to delete it and we will.
  • Guestbook. The name and message you leave are stored in a Google Sheet and shown publicly on the page. Don't post anything you wouldn't want public.
  • High scores. The name or initials you enter go on a public scoreboard next to your score, and are stored on our server.
  • Online games and chat. The name and color you pick are shown to the other people in your game. Chat messages go to the others in the match and are not saved.

what is logged automatically

Like most web servers, ours records each request: your IP address, the page, the time, the page you came from and your browser type. Our game servers also log your IP address when you connect. We use these to keep the site running and to block attacks, and IP addresses that attack the site can be banned automatically. Web server logs are deleted after about 14 days. Game server logs are deleted as they age out, on no fixed schedule.

The visitor counter on the site stores one number, the total, and nothing about who you are.

what stays in your browser

The site does not set cookies of its own. It uses your browser's local storage for things like the jukebox's state, your game settings and saved games, your chat name and the token that reconnects you to a game. None of that is sent to us except the name and token when you play online. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

other services

Using these services sends them your IP address and browser details, under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google stores the email list and guestbook through Apps Script and Sheets, and serves fonts to some pages.
  • YouTube and Bandcamp players load on some music and video pages, and may set their own cookies.
  • The Internet Archive supplies the films on the TV page, played directly from archive.org.
  • MusicBrainz, the Cover Art Archive, the Event Art Archive and The Movie Database supply some archive data and images.
  • cdnjs, jsDelivr and similar serve code libraries to some pages.

Links to other sites are covered by those sites' own policies.

your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete anything we hold that you gave us: your email address, a guestbook message or a score. Tell us the address, name or message so we can find it. We'll answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under your local privacy law, and you can use them the same way.

children

The site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us some, email us and we'll delete it.

changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date below.
