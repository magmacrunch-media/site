cave diving (not even once) for iphone and ipad

Cave Diving (Not Even Once) is a dive into a flooded cave on one tank of air, with your headlamp as the only light. It is built around the song "cave diving (not even once)" by Juanito Thompson, from Spooked (2025), on magmacrunch media, which scores the dive and follows the danger you are in.

questions

how do i play?
Tap DIVE. Drag anywhere on the left two-thirds of the screen to swim, and tap the right third to kick a stroke, which is faster but costs air. Your tank drains the whole time you are under water, and a bite or hitting rock hard costs air too. The pause screen shows the controls.
how do i get my air back?
You don't, not all of it: the whole dive is one tank. Pockets of air in the cave top it up a little, and at a surface you can breathe while you decide, but nothing refills it.
what are the ways out?
Openings where daylight comes down into the cave. Surface at one and you choose: BANK ends the dive and keeps what you carry, times that way out's multiplier, and DIVE ON goes back in. The entrance pays x1, FIRST LIGHT x2, THE CISTERN x3, LAST LIGHT x5 and THE FAR SIDE x8. A fifth way out, THE HIDDEN SPRING, is off the main line, and finding it is up to you.
how is the score worked out?
Each pearl you carry out is 250, plus 1500 if you found at least one pearl and were never hit, all times the multiplier of the way out you bank at. Air left over is not scored. Run out of air and you lose everything you were carrying.
can i pause?
Yes, with the II button at the top right during a dive. The game also pauses itself when you leave the app, and waits.
can i turn the sound off?
Yes: the SOUND button on the title screen turns the music and the sound effects off and on together. The setting shows at the top right during a dive.
why is there a black band on my ipad?
The cave is wide, so the game is played in landscape. Held upright, an iPad shows it with bands above and below; turn it either way round to fill the screen.
where did my dive log go?
The dive log is kept on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. Scores already sent to Game Center stay there.
do i need game center?
No. The whole game works without it. To use the leaderboard and the achievements, sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device; a game center button then appears on the results screen.
does it need the internet?
No. The app plays entirely offline, song included.
can i turn off the vibration?
Yes, with your device's own settings: Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics.
can i play it somewhere else?
Yes, free in a browser at magmacrunch.com/arcade/cave-diving.
