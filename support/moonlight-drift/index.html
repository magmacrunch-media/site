moonlight drift for iphone and ipad

Moonlight Drift is a cave flyer with one control: hold to rise, let go to fall, and dodge the pillars and the edges for as long as you can. It is built around the song "Moonlight Drift" by C.P. Rutledge, and most of its twenty pilots are named for songs and artists on magmacrunch media.

questions

how do i play?
Hold anywhere on the screen to rise and let go to fall. Touching a pillar, the top or the bottom of the cave ends the run. Your score is how many pillars you got past, and every tenth pillar is a milestone, marked with its number.
what are the two modes?
Endless lasts until you touch something. A moonlight set is ninety seconds: get as far as you can before the bar under your score runs out, and fly it to the end to complete the set. Choose on the flight plan before a run. Each mode has its own best scores.
how do i get more pilots?
Four pilots are open from the start. Another opens for every fifteen pillars you clear, counted across all your runs in either mode, so you open all twenty by flying. The picker shows how far each locked pilot is.
do the pilots fly differently?
Many do. The stars on each card in the picker rate its size, agility, control and survival: a small pilot is easier to fit through a gap, and some climb harder or fall slower than others.
who are the pilots?
Most are named for a song, a recording or an artist on magmacrunch media. The rest are the game's own.
why does the moon look different today?
It is tonight's moon. The app reads your device's date and draws the moon in its real phase, and it rises during the first ninety seconds of every run.
why does the cave flash?
The edges of the cave and the stars pulse on the beat of the song. With the sound off, or with Reduce Motion turned on in your device's settings, they stay still.
can i turn the sound off?
Yes: the SFX button on the menu, or in the pilot picker, turns the music and the sound effects off and on together.
it asks me to turn my ipad sideways
The cave is wide, so the game plays in landscape. Turn your iPad either way round.
where did my best runs go?
Best drifts and best sets are kept on your device, so deleting the app deletes them, and your opened pilots with them. Scores already sent to Game Center stay there.
do i need game center?
No. The whole game works without it. To use the two leaderboards and the achievements, sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device; a game center button then appears on your best drifts.
does it need the internet?
No. The app plays entirely offline.
can i turn off the vibration?
Yes, with your device's own settings: Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics.
can i play it somewhere else?
Yes, free in a browser at magmacrunch.com/arcade/moonlight-drift.
