texas hold'em lava dome for iphone and ipad

Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome is solo poker against a dome fifteen songs deep. Each round you are dealt two cards, bet through the flop, the turn and the river, and your best five-card hand has to beat the dome's threshold to go a depth deeper. Each depth plays its own song.

questions

how do i play?
The dome takes an ante, you get two cards and place a bet, and the shared cards come one street at a time. Raise, check or fold at each. Beat the threshold and you win chips and go a depth deeper; miss it and you lose your bet and play the same depth again, at the next round's higher ante. HOW TO PLAY on the start screen has the hand values.
what is the bank?
After each round you can move chips into the bank, where the dome cannot take them. Your score is your bank when the session ends.
can i lose?
Yes. If your chips in play reach zero, the dome wins. You can leave first with ESCAPE DOME, which ends the session with everything banked.
is it real gambling?
No. The chips are the game's own and cannot be bought or exchanged for anything.
where did my high scores go?
High scores are kept on your device, so deleting the app deletes them.
can i turn the music off?
Yes, with MUSIC in the controls under the table. The app remembers your choice.
does it need the internet?
No. The app plays entirely offline.
