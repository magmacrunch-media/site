HOW TO PLAY

the shift ends when the song does

Five machines, and none of them will wait. Tap a station or press its number.

1HOPPER
Pour in flour. Overfill it and it ends up on the floor.
2MIXER
Tap to start, tap again when ready. Leave it and the dough goes tough.
3BELT
Tap to free a jam. It speeds the belt up for a moment.
4OVEN
Pull it golden. Early is raw, late is burnt. Tap fast to put out a fire.
5PACKING
Ship the tray. Four good cookies pay double.

RUSH Four times a shift, on the beat: a faster belt and double for every box.

MESS Spills, burns and fires add up. Fill it and the inspector stops the line for four seconds.

CLEAN FLOOR Finish tidy for a bonus at the whistle.

NO LOSING The shift ends with the song. Stars go by cookies shipped:
