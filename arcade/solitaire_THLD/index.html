How to Play

A solo card game after Texas hold'em and Poker Squares, named for the band Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome, whose songs play at each depth.

The Goal

Survive as long as possible inside the dome. Build your bank before it claims you.

Each Round

  • The dome charges an ante from your chips
  • You receive 2 hole cards, then place your bet
  • The flop, turn, and river are revealed one at a time
  • Raise or check at each street, or fold to cut losses
  • Your best 5-card hand is judged against the dome threshold
  • Beat it: win chips. Miss it: lose your bet

The Bank

After each round, cash out chips into your bank. Banked chips are safe, but chips left in play can win you more. Your final score is your bank total.

Escape or Bust

  • Escape: cash out everything and end the session voluntarily
  • Bust: your chip stack hits zero and the dome wins

Hand Values

  • Royal Flush: 1000 pts / 10x payout
  • Straight Flush: 500 pts / 6x payout
  • Four of a Kind: 250 pts / 4x payout
  • Full House: 150 pts / 3x payout
  • Flush: 100 pts / 2.5x payout
  • Straight: 75 pts / 2x payout
  • Three of a Kind: 50 pts / 1.5x payout
  • Two Pair: 25 pts / 1.25x payout
  • One Pair: 10 pts / 1x payout
  • High Card: cannot beat the dome

The dome threshold rises each round. Antes escalate. Know when to escape.
