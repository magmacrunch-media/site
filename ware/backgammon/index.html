BACKGAMMON

blue screen edition · vs the computer or a friend · doubling cube

Backgammon in the arcade's blue screen edition: a home computer's desktop in blue, white, black and orange, with raised buttons and staircase points.

Play the computer or a friend online with chat, with hitting, the bar, bearing off and a doubling cube. The terminal version plays the same computer opponent.
