BACKGAMMON

neon edition · vs the computer or a friend · doubling cube

Backgammon in the arcade's neon edition: a purple-black board, cyan against magenta, pixel-art pieces and CRT scanlines.

Play the computer or a friend online with chat, with hitting, the bar, bearing off and a doubling cube. The terminal version plays the same computer opponent.
