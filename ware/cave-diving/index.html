CAVE DIVING
(NOT EVEN ONCE)

one song, one tank · your lamp is the only light down there

Swim into a flooded cave with one tank of air and only your headlamp for light. Surface at a daylight opening to bank your pearls, worth up to eight times as much at the far side, or dive on. Drown and you lose them all.

The dive lasts exactly as long as its song, which is cut into a score that follows the danger you are in.
