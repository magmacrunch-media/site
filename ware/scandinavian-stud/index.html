SCANDINAVIAN STUD

skandinavisk stud · five-card stud poker

Five-card stud with one hole card, four face-up cards and four rounds of betting.

Two hands set it apart, the four-card flush and the four-card straight, and both beat a pair. Play three computer opponents or friends online, at a table written in Swedish with English glosses.
