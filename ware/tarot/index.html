FRENCH
TAROT

78-card deck · bidding · the classic french trick-taker

French Tarot for four, you against three computer players, on a custom 78-card magmacrunch deck with a vaporwave card back.

The bids keep their French names, Passe, Prise, Garde, Garde Sans and Garde Contre, beside English labels, and a hand runs through the bidding, the dog and eighteen tricks.
