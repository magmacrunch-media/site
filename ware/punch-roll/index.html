PUNCH//ROLL

a chiptune piano roll, in the spirit of the old versions of FL Studio

▶ Open PUNCH//ROLL lite

Runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

What it does

Each row of the grid is a pitch. Click to put a note down, drag it to move it or stretch it. The sounds are the ones an old game console had: two pulse waves, a triangle and noise. You get up to six tracks, a step sequencer for the drums, and you can load a sample of your own onto any track.

It also does swing, tempo changes in the middle of a song, and tunings with 19, 24 or 31 notes to the octave. Notes go in with the mouse, the computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard, and the scope pops out into its own window if you want it somewhere else.

Lite

This is the demo. Like the old FL Studio demo, it saves your songs but won't open them again, and File > Append is out for the same reason. It exports MP3 and nothing else; the desktop version does WAV, FLAC and Ogg too.

A few things are just how browsers work. Every save downloads a new file. MIDI keyboards only work in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers. The popped-out scope can't stay on top of other windows. Whatever you were working on is still there the next time you open the page.

Built on

magma-kit, with the synth from adenosine. The desktop version isn't out yet.
