The grating is not drawn beside the sound, it is driven by it. The modulator pushes a sound wave through the crystal, and light crossing that moving wave comes out as orders that are FM sidebands of the light itself: order m is bent to its angle and shifted in frequency by m·F. A hard strike is a harder drive and more orders; a higher note or a wider ratio is a finer wave and a wider fan. Land the resting depth on a zero of J0 and the carrier drops out of the sound while the straight-through beam goes dark. The two axes still run out differently: a sideband pushed below zero folds back, and an order past sin θ = 1 stops propagating.
