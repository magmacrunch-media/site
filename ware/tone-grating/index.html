what the symbols mean, and why sound and light agree
m
which component. 0 is the original: the note you played, or the light going straight through. +1 and −1 are the first pair either side of it, +2 and −2 the next pair out, and so on.
cm
how much of the wave ends up in component m. The bars are its size, the table lists it, and its square is that component's share of the power, which is how bright a spot is.
φ, depth
for a phase grating, how far the grooves delay the light at their deepest, in radians (a whole wavelength is 2π). For an amplitude grating, the contrast: 0 is clear glass, 1 is lines that block everything.
fc, fm
the note's pitch, and how often the grating passes. Component m sounds at fc + m·fm.
Λ, λ, θ
the grating's period, the light's wavelength, and the angle a beam leaves at: sin θ = mλ/Λ.

Why they agree. One period of the grating says, point by point, how much light it lets through and how much it delays it. Break that pattern into its Fourier components and you get cm: light leaving in direction m carries cm of the wave. The note is played through the same pattern, swept past fm times a second, and breaking the result into frequencies gives the same cm at fc + m·fm. One pattern, read across space and across time.

Phase and amplitude. A phase grating only delays, so all the light gets through and is redirected: the sine one is FM, the blazed one shifts the whole note up, the lamellar one flips it. An amplitude grating blocks, and what it blocks is gone: the sine one is AM, the Ronchi bars gate the note, and the lost light is the lost loudness.

Where the wavelength comes in. It sets the angle each order leaves at. And for a phase grating it sets the depth: grooves of a fixed height delay blue light by more of a cycle than red, so the grating is deeper for short wavelengths. The depth is set for 500 nm; move the wavelength and the phase gratings change timbre, while the amplitude gratings sound the same in any colour.

Where the two stop matching. A sideband pushed below 0 Hz folds back; an order past sin θ = 1 cannot leave the grating at all. Both are drawn hollow or counted, not hidden.
