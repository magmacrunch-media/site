MAKEME
COOKIES!X4

one song, one shift · five stations · ship cookies

Run a kitsch cookie factory, five stations at once: flour into the hopper, dough out of the mixer, along the belt, golden out of the oven, boxed and shipped.

A shift lasts exactly one play of the song. There is no losing, only how much you shipped and how much mess you left, and make too much mess and the health inspector stops the line.
