2^N

2048 (actually, 2^N) · slide & merge · how far can you go?

2048, generalised. Pick your target, from 2-bit mode at 4 up to 16-bit mode at 65,536, or play endless, with 2048 as the default.

Slide and merge powers of two on a four-by-four board while the tiles heat from icy blue to white hot.
