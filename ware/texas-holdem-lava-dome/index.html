TEXAS HOLD'EM
LAVA DOME

solo hold'em against the dome · bank it or let it ride

Texas Hold'Em with no opponent but the dome. Each round raises the ante and scores your hand against a climbing threshold: beat it to win chips, miss it and lose the bet.

Between hands, bank your chips to keep them or leave them riding, until you go bust or escape the dome.
