GATE//FOLD

an album cover editor, from the square up to the vinyl jacket

▶ Open GATE//FOLD lite

Runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

What it does

Build a cover out of shapes, waveforms, clip art, text and your own photos. Text can run straight or round a curve. Photos come in by button, by dragging them onto the page or by pasting, and the eyedropper can pick colours straight off a reference image you keep beside the cover.

The cover can be 512, 1024, 2048 or 4096 pixels square, and it exports as a PNG.

Lite

The browser version makes square covers and nothing else. It can't save a project to come back to, so finish a cover in one sitting and export it.

The desktop version adds the print side: templates with bleed, folds and crop marks for cassette J-cards, O-cards and shell labels, CD booklets, tray cards and disc labels, vinyl jackets, and floppy and SD card labels, with PDF export and sticker sheets you can print at home. It also has a layers panel, and projects you can save and open again.

Built on

magma-kit. The desktop version isn't out yet.
