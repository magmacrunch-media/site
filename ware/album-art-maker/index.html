    Layers, and this card
    things that are not keys

    Drag the handle above a selection to rotate it. Drop an image on the canvas, or paste one. Click the reference image to sample a colour off it.

    centring

    Drag an element toward the middle and it snaps to the centre of the cover, with a line showing which axis caught. Each axis is separate, so a title can be centred across the cover while staying high on it.

    locking

    A photo fitted with COVER fills the canvas, so every click that misses the text on top of it lands on the photo. Lock it and clicks pass straight through. Lock it from the panel, the layers list, or Ctrl+L.

    the two files

    A .gatefold file is the whole cover: every element, the canvas, and the imported art itself. A PNG is only pixels. Exporting is not saving.
