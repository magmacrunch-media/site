CALDERA

climb the vent · outrun the magma · speed is height

Run, jump and bounce off the walls to climb a volcanic vent while the magma rises under you. Speed turns into height, and jumps that clear several floors chain into a CRUNCH combo.

Crumbling crust and gas vents change the climb. Pick from nine climbers, most of them pilots from Moonlight Drift.
