CHINESE
CHECKERS

8-bit edition · hexagonal star · two to six players

Chinese checkers in the arcade's 8-bit edition, on a home computer's blue text screen, with a hexagonal star of blocks and ten marbles each.

Play two, three, four or six players against the computer, or friends online with room codes and chat. Steps light up green, hops yellow, and hops chain.
