15 PUZZLE

the classic sliding tile puzzle · fewer moves is better

The classic sliding tile puzzle: slide the numbered tiles back into order.

Boards run from the 8 puzzle on three by three up to six by six, with four by four as the default. Your score is your move count, so fewer is better.
