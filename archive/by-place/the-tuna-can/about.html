// The Tuna Can //

The Tuna Can was the affectionate name for the attic of a house on Evans Street in Iowa City. In the years leading up to 2015, when the Tuna Can came into its own, the house was home to a series of party-throwing math grad school classmates. Last among them were Alex and Rolando, a guitarist and a bassist, who met physics students Jake and Stephen at Melrose House in autumn 2014. The quartet started the band Audio Sound Paper (ASP) in early 2015, and began practicing in the Tuna Can that summer. Meanwhile, the four bedroom-house below was passed down to physics student Arya, who invited fellow classmate Jake to join as was moving out of Melrose House. They recruited Iowa City locals Ginny and Jessica, known from the scene at Dog Mansion, to live with them. A noise complaint very early into the lease, called by a very elderly neighbor, led to the house with its inhabitents to be known as Grandma's House.

In addition to ASP, the Tuna Can offered a space for practicing and recording music for other bands in residence including Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome, and dino spumoni.
