There's a very specific vibe that came from downloading a demo version of FL Studio in the mid-'00s, composing something via piano roll in one sitting, and then just rendering an mp3 file without being able to save your work. Inspired by high school classmates The Gaw & Whiteneck Exper1ment, Jake and Bobby started making computer music in 2005 using the name Anabolic Steroids & a Beaver Tranquilizer, in reference to Dodgeball: A True Underdog Story (2004). This was largely a comedic effort, and a way to make D-Rob laugh really hard while driving to school, but it was also a way to explore musical ideas in the days before having much access to recording equipment. The track Kinda Reminiscent of Millstone is an example of this, with a direct musical reference to Stalemate by Millstone that flows into one of Jake's first original compositions.

AS&BT later inspired the work of Jimmi in 2008...
