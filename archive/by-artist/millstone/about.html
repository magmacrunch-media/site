Millstone was a rock band from Southern New Hampshire, together from the fall of 2003 to the spring of 2004: Dave Hayes and Dave Robinson on guitar and vocals, Jake McCoy on drums, and Keith Galvin on bass and vocals. Their own website described them in two words: "Simply rock."

It started when Jake and Dave got together on a whim to jam. The two Daves began playing together, then joined up with Jake and practiced at his house almost every day. They took the name from Millstone Road, the street Jake's house, Frogwood Manor, is on, and played their first show there on Halloween night 2003, for the neighborhood. Keith heard about the band through Hayes and took over bass on December 10.

In about six months they wrote fifteen original songs. Practice takes of Stalemate and A New Hope were recorded on December 15, and Hayes and Robinson recorded an acoustic Nothing Society on Robinson's new 4-track on December 27. A demo was planned more than once, and the site never says it was finished. They covered Weezer's The Christmas Song and Saves the Day's Rocks Tonic Juice Magic.

They played Searles Castle in Windham on January 22, 2004, and entered the Salem High School Battle of the Bands, missing the prelims. The last news post, on April 24, 2004, says practice had stopped for a while.

Stalemate turns up again years later: Fruity Loops Debauchery Bros. quote it in Kinda Reminiscent of Millstone.
