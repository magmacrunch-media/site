The story of magmacrunch is perhaps best told in a non-linear fashion. Let's first take a step back to late 2015, in Iowa City, Iowa. The post space rock band Texas Hold'Em Lava Dome (THLD) was formed by a Venn diagram of brothers and physics grad school classmates. Members Jake, Jon, Stephen, and Judah would record music in the The Tuna Can. House inhabitent Jessica would attend their shows, frequently shouting "TEXASTOAST MAGMACRUNCH!", as if to grant the band an alter-ego. Amongst discussions of atomic physics, Stephen and Jake would often talk about the idea of a creative collective at the bar of the beloved Trumpet Blossom Cafe in Iowa City.

McCoy brothers Jake, James, and Jon grew up playing music at Frogwood Manor in a forested neighborhood of New Hampshire during the late 1990s and the 2000s. Across the street was a young C.P. Rutledge, who grew up playing music at Spirit Castle. With the two Daves, Bobito, and other musicians living on the same block, this micro-scene was home to a host of bands and projects over the years. These include Millstone, Vinny Bobarino, Juanito Thompson, The Four B's, Alien Hunting, and Bottle Boys, along with solo material by Brother Dynamo and Jon/Woah. School and work later took Jake and Jon to Iowa City, where they played in THLD and other associated projects. Meanwhile, James played in the Philadelphia-based band The Evergreen Drive with college friends at Temple.

Newer acts have since joined the long-time members, among them Dag Henderson and, most recently, Pennsylvania's Sex Van Floor Plan. Stephen, who played in Audio Sound Paper with Jake while THLD was active, went on to form Murder Finger with Jon, and Puma Man in the late 2010s, and most recently played in dogging in parked cars, a two-piece post-rock group in Spain. In addition to music and field recordings, he joined the magmacrunch development team along with Jake and James in 2026.

The archive is built on MusicBrainz, the open music encyclopedia: every artist, place and label here has an entry there, and the releases, recordings, works and shows these pages list come from it. The write-ups and photos are the site's own.
