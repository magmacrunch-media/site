Welcome to the archive. Bear with us, as much of this is still under construction. Our intention is to tell the story of the broader magmacrunch collective through documenting the artists, places, and labels that have been involved, even if just tangentially or historically. As such, this includes entries that contain music that was released elsewhere, or before magmacrunch media was founded. Much of the archive is built on MusicBrainz, the open music encyclopedia, using data entered by magmacrunch. In addition to narrative writing, it also features links, photography, and other media associated with each entry.
